About

Vi Saint (Viktor Sekovski) is a Creative Technologist working across design, code and visual storytelling. His practice explores how thoughtful interfaces, memorable identities and carefully crafted interactions can make technology feel more human, intuitive and emotionally engaging.

Working remotely with clients across industries, he designs and builds digital experiences that balance clarity with character. Whether creating e-commerce platforms, editorial websites or experimental interfaces, his focus remains the same: translating complexity into products that people genuinely enjoy using.
